Privacy Policy for SM SMP

Last Updated: July 25, 2026

SM SMP ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, protection, and disclosure of information when you access our Minecraft server (play.smsmp.net), our website (smsmp.net), our webstore (smsmp.fluxstore.net), and our Discord community (discord.smsmp.net) (collectively, the "Services").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing or using our Services, you consent to the data practices described in this document.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
We collect information that you provide to us directly, as well as data that is collected automatically when you use our Services.
1.1. Game Data: When you join our Minecraft server, we automatically log your Minecraft Username, Unique User ID (UUID), IP Address, login/logout timestamps, chat logs, and in-game statistics (e.g., playtime, economy balances, inventory, kills).
1.2. Payment Information: When you make a purchase on our webstore, you must provide your Minecraft Username. Payment processing is handled entirely by our third-party provider, FluxStore, and their associated payment gateways (e.g., PayPal, Stripe). We DO NOT collect, process, or store your sensitive financial information (such as credit card numbers or bank details). We only receive transaction confirmations and order identifiers to deliver your virtual goods.
1.3. Communication Data: If you interact with our Discord server or contact us via email, we collect your Discord username, Discord User ID, email address, and the contents of your messages.
1.4. Website Usage Data: When you visit our website, we may automatically collect standard web server logs, which include your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, and the pages you view. This data is collected strictly for the purposes of website updates, continuous improvements, and fraud detection.
1.5. Screenshare and Inspection Data: As detailed in our Terms of Service, we reserve the right to perform screenshares (SS) or inspections on users suspected of using unauthorized modifications. During these inspections, our staff may temporarily view files, folders, processes, and applications on your local device. We do not permanently copy, store, or distribute this local data; it is viewed solely in real-time for the purpose of verifying rule compliance.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
We use the collected information for the following legitimate business purposes:
2.1. Service Delivery: To operate the Minecraft server, authenticate your identity, and instantly deliver purchased Virtual Goods to your in-game account.
2.2. Enforcement and Security: To monitor server activity, detect fraudulent transactions, track alternate accounts, perform anti-cheat inspections (screenshares), and enforce our Terms of Service.
2.3. Community Features: To display competitive leaderboards on our website utilizing your in-game statistics.
2.4. Customer Support: To respond to your inquiries, support tickets, and purchase disputes.
2.5. Improvement: To analyze traffic and usage patterns to optimize server performance and improve our website's user experience.

3. HOW WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION
We respect your privacy and DO NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. We may share your information only under the following circumstances:
3.1. Service Providers: We may share necessary data with trusted third-party vendors who assist us in operating our Services (such as server hosting providers, DDoS protection services, and our webstore provider, FluxStore). These providers are bound by their own privacy policies.
3.2. Legal Compliance: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, subpoena, or court order, or if we believe in good faith that such action is necessary to comply with legal processes.
3.3. Protection of Rights: We may disclose information to investigate fraud, defend against legal claims, or protect the rights, property, and safety of SM SMP, our staff, or our players.

4. COOKIES AND TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES
Our website may use "cookies" (small data files stored on your device) to enhance user experience, save session preferences, and analyze site traffic. You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, if you do not accept cookies, some portions of our website (such as the webstore cart) may not function properly.

5. DATA RETENTION
We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the Services. We may retain certain data (such as transaction records, IP logs, and ban histories) indefinitely to resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and enforce our agreements.

6. DATA SECURITY
We implement industry-standard administrative and technical security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
Our Services are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without such consent, we will take reasonable steps to delete that information.

8. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS
SM SMP operates globally. Your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in countries other than your own, where data protection laws may differ. By using our Services, you consent to the transfer of your information to our server locations.

9. YOUR RIGHTS
Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, please contact our support team.
